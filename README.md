🌞 EcoSolar
Residential Solar Solutions for Bulacan and Nearby Towns
EcoSolar is a locally driven solar installation team focused on delivering safe, reliable, and properly documented residential solar power systems.
We help homeowners reduce electricity bills, protect their homes, and transition confidently to clean energy.

🌱 Our Mission
To make solar energy accessible, safe, and honest for Filipino households—starting in Baliuag, Bulacan and nearby towns—by applying disciplined engineering, proper certification, and long‑term customer support.

👥 Who We Are
EcoSolar is built by a multidisciplinary team with decades of real‑world experience:

Certified Solar Technician (in training) – Former electronics educator with strong theoretical and practical foundations in PV systems.
Information Technology Specialist – Handles system monitoring, documentation, and performance tracking.
Maritime Professional (27 years sea service) – Brings international safety standards, risk management, and strict operating procedures into every installation.

Together, we combine technical accuracy, safety discipline, and system thinking—qualities often missing in informal solar installations.

🔧 What We Do
We specialize in residential rooftop solar systems, including:

Grid‑tied solar installations (bawas‑bill systems)
System design and load assessment
Safe mounting and roof protection
Proper grounding and electrical compliance
Net metering documentation assistance
System testing and commissioning
After‑sales support and maintenance guidance

Our focus is quality over quantity. Every system is designed specifically for the home it serves.
